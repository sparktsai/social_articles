Case Study — Case A, Study 2

Looking at the same case from a software engineering perspective, I see a few current conditions:

1. Multiple tools are used across the engineering pipeline.
2. Different stages have very different levels of AI adoption.
3. Dependency on AI is becoming deeper.
4. Engineering roles are changing, with responsibilities expanding upstream and becoming more connected to AI.
5. DevOps is entering an awkward transition as traditional responsibility boundaries begin to shift.
6. Engineering benefits appear uneven, with multiple peaks and valleys rather than a smooth linear curve.

These are observations from one real enterprise case, not conclusions.
