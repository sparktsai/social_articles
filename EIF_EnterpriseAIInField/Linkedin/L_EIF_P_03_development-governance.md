Case Study — Case A, Study 3

Looking at the same case from a development governance perspective, I see a few current conditions:

1. An SDLC appears to be in place.
2. The current SDLC does not yet appear to be an AI-specific SDLC.
3. Software development has processes and controls, but no clearly articulated governance model is visible.
4. Company policies and engineering responsibilities are gradually changing. Whether the organizational structure is also changing is unclear.
5. AI adoption is already affecting development practices, but an explicit governance concept is not yet visible across the development lifecycle.

These are observations from one enterprise case, not a maturity assessment.
