Case Study — Case B, Study 2

Looking at the same case from a governance perspective, I see a few current conditions:

1. The current AI web chat has a relatively narrow role when it answers approved product and FAQ questions.
2. The planned AI call center may need access to customer identity, interaction history, tickets, and product information.
3. Answering, accessing data, taking action, and escalating to a human are different capabilities that may require different controls.
4. Historical ticket resolutions are useful evidence, but they are not automatically approved knowledge for AI to reuse with customers.
5. Knowledge ownership, version control, error correction, and accountability become more important as AI moves beyond FAQ answering.
6. Customer-service governance therefore extends across channels, systems, knowledge, actions, and human escalation—not only the AI model.

These are observations from one enterprise case, not a governance maturity assessment.
