Case Study — Case B, Study 1

Looking at the same case from an information-integration perspective, I see a few current conditions:

1. Customer-service information exists across the website, FAQ, ticket system, call center, and live chat.
2. Customer-service agents often assemble the information they need from several systems during one customer interaction.
3. A resolved issue does not automatically become approved, reusable knowledge.
4. Some solutions remain in tickets, some remain in agent experience, and only some are reviewed into FAQs or product information.
5. The current AI web chat mainly uses approved product and FAQ content, then escalates unsupported questions to a human agent or ticket process.
6. Adding AI does not automatically integrate the historical information already distributed across the customer-service stack.

These are observations from one enterprise case, not conclusions about the quality of its systems.
