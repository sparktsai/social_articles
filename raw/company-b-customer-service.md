# Company B Customer Service Case - Source Notes

Company B is a physical-product sales company.

Its customer-service capability evolved in layers:

1. Product information and FAQs were first published on the web.
2. A packaged ticket system was introduced for issue reporting and tracking.
3. A call center was added and connected to the ticket system.
4. Live chat was introduced.
5. AI web chat and AI FAQ were introduced this year.
6. An AI call center is planned for next year.

The Case 2 Article must clearly describe:

- What information exists in each system.
- How customer-service agents handle issues in daily work.
- How new knowledge is added.
- How the current AI customer service connects to existing systems.
- The governance questions the CIO is considering.
- The ROI questions the CEO must report to the board.

Planned follow-up studies:

1. Information integration across legacy systems.
2. Governance viewpoint.
3. ROI, including customer satisfaction and labor cost.
